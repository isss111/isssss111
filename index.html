Women's health is the largest underinvested market in medicine.

Inês Santos Silva builds companies and investment vehicles to close the funding, data, and care gaps that have kept it that way, from preventive primary care to ovarian aging and longevity science.

Now

  • Co-Founder & COO TejoMed

    Portugal's first membership-based family medicine clinic, where prevention is the clinical philosophy and the doctor-patient relationship is built to last decades, not minutes. TejoMed is reshaping how people access primary care.

  • Core Team Member AthenaBIO

    An investment platform backing early-stage biotech in women's health and ovarian aging. The thesis is straightforward: women's health is chronically underfunded and overlooked, and that mismatch makes it one of the most compelling market opportunities in biotech and pharma today. Women are actively seeking solutions, and the science is finally catching up.

  • Co-Founder Portuguese Women in Tech

    Bringing more women into the industry. Inês also organizes its Women & Wealth and Women & Leadership conferences.

  • Non-Executive Board Member Almedina

    One of Portugal's most established publishers.

Background

Inês is an alumna of the Singularity University Graduate Studies Program (2013) and THNK School of Creative Leadership (2014), and a Marshall Memorial Fellow (2019) of the German Marshall Fund.

She reads widely across business, history, and science and shares her reading on Goodreads.
