Inês Santos Silva

Women's health is the largest underinvested market in medicine. Inês Santos Silva builds companies and investment vehicles to close the funding, data, and care gaps that have kept it that way, from preventive primary care to ovarian aging and longevity science.

She is Co-Founder and COO of TejoMed, Portugal's first membership-based family medicine clinic, where prevention is the clinical philosophy and the doctor-patient relationship is built to last decades, not minutes. TejoMed is reshaping how people access primary care.

She is also a Core Team Member of AthenaBIO, an investment platform backing early-stage biotech in women's health and ovarian aging. AthenaBIO's thesis is straightforward: women's health is chronically underfunded and overlooked, and that mismatch makes it one of the most compelling market opportunities in biotech and pharma today. Women are actively seeking solutions, and the science is finally catching up.

Separately, Inês co-founded Portuguese Women in Tech to bring more women into the industry, and organizes its Women & Wealth and Women & Leadership conferences.

She serves as a Non-Executive Board Member at Almedina, one of Portugal's most established publishers.

Inês is an alumna of the Singularity University Graduate Studies Program (2013) and THNK School of Creative Leadership (2014), and a Marshall Memorial Fellow (2019) of the German Marshall Fund.

She reads widely across business, history, and science and shares her reading on Goodreads.
